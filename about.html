About Gulf Tools Hub

Gulf Tools Hub started from a simple frustration — every time someone in the UAE needed to calculate gratuity, check VAT on an invoice, or work out an EMI payment, they had to either use a generic global calculator that did not understand local rules, or spend time searching through complicated government PDFs.

So we built the tool we wished existed: a clean, fast, UAE-specific calculator hub designed for how people in the Gulf actually work and live.

Our mission: Make everyday financial calculations instant, accurate, and free for every UAE resident and expat — with zero friction, no signup, and no hidden costs.

What Gulf Tools Hub offers

35+ free calculators covering the most common financial and practical questions for UAE residents:

  • UAE Finance — VAT, Gratuity, Salary Breakdown, Leave Salary, Overtime, Fuel Cost, Rent Affordability
  • Loans & investment — EMI, Loan Calculator, Mortgage Affordability, Savings, ROI, Compound Interest
  • Everyday utilities — Age Calculator, Date Difference, Days Until, Time Duration, Unit Converter
  • Text tools — Word Counter, Case Converter, Password Generator, Slug Generator, and more

Why it is free

Gulf Tools Hub is free because that is the whole point. UAE residents — especially the millions of expats navigating a foreign financial system — should not have to pay to understand their own gratuity entitlement or VAT obligations. Every tool on this site will remain free to use, forever.

How the tools work

Every calculation runs entirely in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server. Your salary figures, loan amounts, and personal details never leave your device — making every tool instant, even on a slow connection.
