How EMI Is Calculated

Understand the formula behind monthly installment payments and what changes the final amount.

EMI stands for equal monthly installment. It is one of the most searched finance terms because people want to know a single practical answer: how much will I pay every month?

As interest rates rise, the EMI increases. As the term length increases, the EMI may drop, but the total interest paid often rises.
