How to Calculate VAT in the UAE

A practical guide to adding and removing 5% VAT correctly for pricing, invoices, and estimates.

Understanding VAT in the UAE is useful for businesses, freelancers, and even shoppers who want a quick way to confirm totals. The standard concept is simple: when you want to add VAT, you calculate 5% of the net amount and add it to the base price. When you want to remove VAT from a VAT-inclusive total, you divide by 1.05.

For example, if your base amount is AED 1,000, the VAT is AED 50 and the gross amount is AED 1,050. If the total is AED 1,050 and you want to know the net amount before VAT, dividing AED 1,050 by 1.05 gives AED 1,000.
