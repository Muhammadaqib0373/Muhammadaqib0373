UAE Gratuity Explained

A simple overview of gratuity estimation and why basic salary matters more than total package.

The UAE gratuity topic attracts strong search demand because it directly affects career decisions, resignations, and financial planning. Most people want a fast estimate before speaking with HR or reviewing contract details.

The simplified estimate usually starts with the employee’s monthly basic salary, not the full package. A daily basic salary is approximated by dividing the monthly basic salary by 30.
