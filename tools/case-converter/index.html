Frequently asked questions

Does the tool keep formatting?

It keeps the raw text content but changes the letter casing.

Is title case the same as sentence case?

No. Title case capitalizes major words, while sentence case capitalizes sentence starts.

Can this work offline after load?

Yes. It is a client-side text transformation.
