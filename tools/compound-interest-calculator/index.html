How this tool works

The future value combines growth on the starting balance with growth on any monthly contributions. Because contribution timing can vary in real life, treat the result as a practical estimate rather than a bank quote.

Frequently asked questions

Does this guarantee returns?

No. It is only a projection using the rate you enter.

What rate should I use?

Use a rate that matches your product or your planning assumption, then test a few scenarios.

Can I leave monthly contribution at zero?

Yes. The tool will then show growth on the starting amount only.
