How this tool works

This is a manual-rate converter, which means you control the exchange rate. That is useful when you are comparing cash exchange counters, checking invoice conversions, or using a company-set accounting rate instead of a live market feed.

Frequently asked questions

Does it use live market data?

No. You must enter the rate yourself.

Why show the inverse rate?

It helps you sanity-check whether the rate direction is correct.

Can I swap currencies quickly?

Yes. Use the swap button to flip the labels and conversion direction.
