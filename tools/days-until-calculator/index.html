How this tool works

The calculator compares a starting date with your target date and returns the gap in multiple formats. If the target date is in the future, you will see a countdown. If it is in the past, you will see how many days ago it happened.

Frequently asked questions

Can I count from a custom date?

Yes. Change the Count from field to any date.

What are weekdays?

Weekdays are Monday to Friday and exclude Saturdays and Sundays.

Can I use this for past dates?

Yes. The result will tell you how many days ago the date was.
