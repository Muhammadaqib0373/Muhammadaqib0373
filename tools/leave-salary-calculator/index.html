How this tool works

Different employers and internal policies may use different day-rate bases when estimating leave-related pay. This tool lets you test more than one approach instead of forcing a single formula.

Use it as a quick planning estimate only and confirm the exact method from your contract, HR team, or company policy.

Frequently asked questions

Why are there multiple day-rate options?

Organizations sometimes divide salary by 30, 26, or another internal day count.

Should I enter total salary or basic salary?

For planning, start with monthly basic salary unless your policy specifically uses another figure.

Is this an official legal calculation?

No. It is a simple estimate and should be checked against your actual policy.
