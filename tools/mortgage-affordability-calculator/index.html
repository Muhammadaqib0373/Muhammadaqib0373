How this tool works

The calculator sets a monthly housing budget based on your chosen income ratio, subtracts current debt commitments, and then estimates the loan size that budget could support using a standard amortization formula.

This is still a planning tool, not a bank approval. Lenders may use stricter rules, fees, insurance, credit checks, and property-specific limits.

Frequently asked questions

What is the housing ratio?

It is the share of monthly income you are comfortable putting toward the mortgage payment.

Why subtract existing debts?

Existing loans and card payments reduce the budget available for housing.

Does the estimate include fees?

No. Add your own buffer for registration fees, insurance, and other closing costs.
