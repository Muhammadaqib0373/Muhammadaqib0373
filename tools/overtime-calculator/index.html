How this tool works

The page derives a rough hourly rate from monthly salary using a 30-day month and your chosen standard daily hours, then applies the overtime multiplier to the extra hours entered.

Use it for quick planning only. Actual overtime treatment can depend on employer policy, contract terms, timing, and local rules.

Frequently asked questions

Why choose a multiplier?

Different policies may use different overtime rates for normal days, nights, or holidays.

Can I use basic salary instead of total salary?

Yes, if that is the figure your company uses for overtime calculations.

Is this legally exact?

No. It is a quick estimate for planning and comparison.
