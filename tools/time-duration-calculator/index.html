How this tool works

This calculator converts both times into minutes, subtracts any break, and then shows the result as hours and minutes. If your shift or task passes midnight, tick the overnight option.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use this for work shifts?

Yes. Add a break if needed and use the overnight option for night shifts.

Does it show decimal hours?

Yes. You can choose the decimal precision from the dropdown.

What if the break is longer than the shift?

The calculator will ask you to reduce the break minutes.
