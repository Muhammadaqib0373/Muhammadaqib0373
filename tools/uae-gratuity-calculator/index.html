How this tool works

This UAE gratuity calculator is designed as a practical estimator for employees who want a fast answer before doing a formal HR check. End-of-service benefit pages usually attract high-intent search traffic because users are often making financial decisions around resignation, job changes, or final settlement planning.

The simplified estimate usually starts with the employee’s monthly basic salary, not the full package. A daily basic salary is approximated by dividing the monthly basic salary by 30. Then an estimated number of gratuity days is applied depending on service length.
