How this tool works

The UAE VAT calculator is one of the most commercially valuable tools you can publish because people search for it with strong intent. Businesses, freelancers, and shoppers use it to check invoice totals, estimate tax on quotations, and confirm net amounts after VAT.

For adding VAT, multiply the original amount by 0.05 to get the tax, then add that value to the base amount. For removing VAT from a VAT-inclusive total, divide the gross amount by 1.05.

Frequently asked questions

How do I add VAT in the UAE?

Multiply the amount by 5% to get the VAT, then add it to the base amount.

How do I remove VAT from a total?

Divide the VAT-inclusive amount by 1.05 to get the net amount before VAT.

Can I use this for quotations and invoices?

Yes. It works for quick estimates, quotations, and invoice checks.
